# Badal Khandelwal — Portfolio

> I think in products, design for people, and build what I believe should exist.

Personal portfolio of **Badal Khandelwal**, focused on product design, product building, and full-stack development.

This portfolio is being designed and built as a product itself — with the goal of showing not only what I build, but how I think about problems, usability, design decisions, and turning ideas into working products.

---

## 🎯 Purpose

This portfolio is designed for three primary audiences:

1. **Product & Design Recruiters**
2. **Startup Founders looking for product designers/builders**
3. **Freelance clients looking for products designed and built**

The goal is not to make the portfolio feel like a traditional résumé website.

Instead, it should communicate:

> **This person doesn't just design interfaces. He thinks about the product, the people using it, and how to actually build it.**

---

## 🧠 Design Philosophy

My approach to product design is centered around **usability and product thinking**, rather than interfaces alone.

I am interested in understanding:

- What problem are we actually solving?
- Who is experiencing the problem?
- Why does the problem matter?
- What should the product do?
- How should people interact with it?
- What should be automated?
- What should remain controlled?
- How can the idea be turned into a working product?
- What can we learn after building it?

The portfolio is designed to show these decisions through projects and case studies rather than simply listing skills.

## 🎨 Visual Direction

The visual identity is built around two themes.

### Night Theme

- Black
- Charcoal
- Grey
- Off-white
- Subtle grey textures
- Minimal contrast
- No purple/neon aesthetics

### Day Theme

- Warm cream
- Off-white
- Charcoal
- Muted grey

The overall visual language aims to feel:

**Editorial × Product Design × Technology**

The design should remain minimal, premium, intentional, and usable rather than relying on excessive visual effects.
